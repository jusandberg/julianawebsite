When to use formative and summative assessment

Formative assessment helps people improve while they learn. Summative assessment shows what they can do at the end. In five minutes, see the difference and practise picking the right one.

The goal

Explain the difference between formative and summative assessment, recognise each in real situations, and choose the right one for what you need.

Why each activity looks the way it does

  1. Myth or fact. Most people sort assessments by format ("it's a quiz"). Starting there shifts the focus to purpose.
  2. Compare. Each type is shown with when, stakes and purpose, plus a real example, so learners can tell them apart quickly.
  3. Formative or summative? Six workplace moments with an explanation after each, so learners practise the exact judgement they'll use when designing.

How I would measure it

Level 1A one-question pulse: how confident do you feel choosing the right assessment type now?
Level 2Practice score, and which moments people miss most often.
Level 3A 30-day check-in: did the next program plan include low-stakes practice before the final check?
Level 4Pass rates and time to proficiency on programs designed with both types.
