Leading with emotional intelligence.

New managers don't get tripped up by strategy. They get tripped up by the emotionally charged moment, the one that calls for a response before logic catches up. Step into three of them and choose how you'd lead.

The throughline

  • The pause between feeling and acting is where leadership lives. Name what you feel before you decide what to do.
  • Empathy isn't agreement. You can understand someone's experience and still hold a line.
  • Under stress, the emotionally intelligent move is almost always to slow down, not speed up.
