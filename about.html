About me

Oh, hey there!

I’m Juliana, I guide humans back to their essence; not by becoming someone new.

The essence of my work lives in what remains when the masks drop, when performing stops, and when you meet yourself as you truly are: unedited, unarmored, unhardened.

U are the center.

Mission

Create space to return.

Helping humans reconnect with wholeness and realign with the self beneath the noise.

Vision

A collective return to essence.

People living, choosing, and relating from clarity, honesty, and inner ground.

Values

Warm, honest, and grounded.

Compassionate honesty, self-trust, and grounded movement that carries into real life.

My story

How I got here.

Learning & development

I came to coaching through learning and development, after years designing programs that help people lead, communicate, and navigate change inside fast-moving tech companies.

What I noticed

The most meaningful growth rarely came from another framework or training. It came when someone felt safe enough to slow down, hear themselves clearly, and trust what they found.

Why coaching

Coaching is where that work gets personal. It is a structured conversation that helps you see yourself clearly, reconnect with your own knowing, and take grounded action at your pace.
