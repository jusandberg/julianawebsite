The goal

Describe the three phases of SAM, explain why early prototypes are rough on purpose, and choose the SAM-friendly move in common project moments.

Why each activity looks the way it does

  1. Myth or fact. People often hear "agile" and assume SAM skips analysis. Clearing that up first keeps the rest from being misread.
  2. The three phases. Learners open each phase themselves and hear what it sounds like on a real project, not just a definition.
  3. What would you do? Four real project moments with tempting wrong answers, each explained, so the model sticks as a set of decisions.

How I would measure it

Level 1A one-question pulse at the end: how likely are you to try a rough prototype on your next project?
Level 2Practice score, plus which moment people miss most often.
Level 3A 30-day check-in: did the next project include an early prototype review?
Level 4Time from kickoff to first stakeholder feedback, before and after.

How it's built

Hand-built in HTML, CSS and JavaScript, with no tools or templates. It works on phones, with a keyboard, and with reduced motion turned on.
