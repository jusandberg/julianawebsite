Gradients

Quiet movement between brand tones.

Use one gradient consistently within a feature. Slate to gold is the primary treatment for tool icons and signature brand moments.

Icons

Round, quiet, line-based.

Use simple stroke icons in circular containers. Keep them functional and minimal.

Service

Focused coaching

Cards stay white, crisp, and quiet. Add one accent marker, not many competing decorations.

Resource

Reflection guide

Resources can use tags, simple icons, and short summary copy for scannability.

Quote

Client reflection

Testimonials should feel spacious. Keep quote cards uncluttered and human.

Imagery

Portraits are circular and calm.

Use real, clear photography with simple backgrounds. Avoid square frames for portraits in this direction.

Motion

Detached floating circles.

Decorations should drift softly around the edges and never cover text or faces.
