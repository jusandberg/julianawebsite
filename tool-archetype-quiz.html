Which inner force is guiding you right now?

This reflective coaching quiz explores three inner modes that may become more active at different moments: The Witness, The Inner Guide, and The Explorer. It is a prompt for self-inquiry, not a psychological diagnosis or fixed personality type.

Where the idea comes from: The language of archetypes is inspired by Carl Jung's view of recurring symbolic patterns, while these three names are a coaching synthesis informed by mindfulness, intuition-based reflection, and experiential learning. They are not three archetypes defined by Jung, and the quiz is not a validated psychometric assessment.

The Witness

Presence · awareness · compassion. Notices thoughts and feelings without immediately becoming them.

The Inner Guide

Intuition · stillness · inner wisdom. Listens for values, bodily signals, and quiet knowing.

The Explorer

Courage · wonder · trusting the unknown. Learns through movement, experimentation, and discovery.
