Coaching explained

What Is Coaching?

Coaching is a reflective, forward-moving conversation designed to help you access deeper clarity, make aligned decisions, and strengthen your self-leadership.

It’s not about giving advice or telling you what to do. It’s a collaborative process rooted in curiosity, powerful questions, and evidence-informed developmental practices.

Who it supports

Who Is Coaching For?

Coaching is for individuals who want to understand themselves more deeply and move through life with greater alignment.

When you want to understand yourself more deeply.

Coaching helps you notice the patterns, needs, values, and inner signals shaping how you move through your life.

You do not need to be struggling to benefit from coaching — you simply need curiosity, openness, and a desire for greater alignment.

Helpful distinctions

Coaching versus therapy.

Coaching
  • Future-focused and goal-oriented
  • Centered on self-awareness, clarity, and patterns
  • Collaborative and non-directive
  • Supports action, integration, and self-leadership
  • Not clinical, diagnostic, or treatment-based
Versus
Therapy
  • Focuses on healing, mental health, and processing past or unresolved experiences
  • Delivered by licensed mental health professionals
  • May involve diagnosis, treatment, or clinical intervention

Coaching and therapy both support personal growth, but they serve different purposes.
