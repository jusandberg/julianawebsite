      The 1-minute version

      What this case study shows

      Who it's for
      The whole company, from individual contributors to senior leaders.
      The idea
      Leadership programs work better when they're tied to job architecture, performance reviews, internal mobility and succession, so people learn and get reviewed against the same expectations.
      The programs
      Individual contributors choose a six-week leadership track or a six-week craft track, each with three months of mentorship. Managers at each level take an eight-week program.
      How I build it
      The whole build takes under three months. I use SAM, so leaders see a working pilot within the first two months.
      How I measure it
      After every cohort I review feedback, skills checks and manager check-ins, alongside engagement, internal mobility, attrition and succession.
