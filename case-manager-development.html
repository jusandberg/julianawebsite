How I build leadership development for a whole company

Individual contributors get two six-week tracks, one to try leading people and one to grow in their craft, each with three months of mentorship. Managers at every level get an eight-week program, and managers hired from outside get a 90-day start. All of it ties to job levels, reviews, internal moves and succession, and the build takes under three months.
