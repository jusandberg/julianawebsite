Leadership development that's connected to how people grow

A sample model for building leaders at every stage: two tracks for individual contributors, one toward leadership and one deeper into their craft, then short programs for managers. It's all tied to job architecture, performance, internal mobility and succession planning.

This is a sample model that shows how I'd approach the work. The levels, timelines and measures are examples, and they aren't results from a past program.

Who it's for
Individual contributors, new managers, experienced managers and senior leaders
Format
6 weeks plus mentorship for individual contributors, 8 weeks for each manager level
Connected to
Job architecture, performance reviews, internal moves and succession
My role
Needs analysis, program architecture, design, stakeholder alignment and measurement
