First principles

Break a problem down to what you know is true. Then build back up from there, instead of copying what was done before.

You'll see it when

Every plan looks like last year's plan, because "that's how we do it."

Ask yourself

What do we know for sure, and what are we just assuming?

Seeing the situation · 2 of 7

The map is not the territory

Reports, dashboards and org charts are simple pictures of the work. They're useful, and they always leave things out.

You'll see it when

The report keeps saying "on track," and the team keeps missing deadlines.

Ask yourself

What is this picture leaving out?

Making the choice · 3 of 7

Inversion

Instead of asking how to make something work, ask what would make it fail. Then plan to avoid those things.

You'll see it when

Launches keep tripping over the same few surprises.

Ask yourself

If this failed six months from now, what would the reason be?

The 80/20 rule

A small part of the work usually drives most of the results. Find that part and protect time for it.

You'll see it when

The team is busy all week, and the numbers barely move.

Ask yourself

Which few things on this list make the biggest difference?

Making the choice · 5 of 7

Opportunity cost

Every yes is a no to something else. The real cost of a choice is the best option you gave up to make it.

You'll see it when

New requests keep getting added, and the important project keeps slipping.

Ask yourself

If we say yes to this, what are we saying no to?

Thinking ahead · 6 of 7

Second-order thinking

Keep asking "and then what?" The first result of a decision is rarely the last one, and the later ones are often bigger.

You'll see it when

Quick fixes keep creating new problems a few months later.

Ask yourself

And then what happens? And after that?

Feedback loops

Some effects feed back into their own causes, so the problem keeps growing. To break the loop, change one link in it.

You'll see it when

The busiest people keep getting busier.

Ask yourself

Where does this loop back on itself, and which link can we change?
