The frameworks behind it

Evidence-based models keep my work rigorous. The ones I reach for, and why each earns its place.

Instructional Design

ADDIE

The classic five-phase model (Analyze, Design, Develop, Implement, Evaluate) that gives every project a rigorous, end-to-end structure. My backbone for large-scale programs like onboarding redesigns.

Agile Design

SAM

The Successive Approximation Model replaces a linear waterfall with rapid cycles of prototyping, review, and iteration. My go-to in fast-moving environments where feedback early and often matters.

Cognitive Hierarchy

Bloom's Taxonomy

A hierarchy of cognitive skills, from remembering and understanding up through applying, analyzing, evaluating, and creating. It shapes how I write objectives and assessments.

Evaluation

Kirkpatrick

Four levels of training evaluation: Reaction, Learning, Behavior, and Results. It connects what happens in the room to what changes on the job, at Levels 3 and 4.

Development Mix

70-20-10

Designing leadership pathways as full ecosystems: stretch assignments and peer coaching alongside formal sessions. Most growth happens on the job, and the design should reflect that.

Books I love.

Some of my favourite instructional design and talent development books. The ones that shaped how I think, design, and lead.

Cammy Bean

The Accidental Instructional Designer

Packed with tools and insight for those of us who stumbled into instructional design. Relatable, practical, and genuinely fun to read.

Maria Morukian

Diversity, Equity, and Inclusion for Trainers

An essential tool for L&D professionals who want DEI principles to be more than a checkbox. This is how you build it into the curriculum itself.

Julie Dirksen

Design for How People Learn

Clear, evidence-based insights on memory, attention, and motivation. Changed the way I think about what "learning" actually means.

Cathy Moore

Map It

Action mapping keeps every project anchored to a real business goal and what people need to do, not just know. I return to this constantly.

Brown, Roediger & McDaniel

Make It Stick

The science of durable learning: retrieval practice, spacing, interleaving. It changed how I design every single course.

Brené Brown

Dare to Lead

Courage, vulnerability, and trust reframed as leadership skills you can actually teach. A cornerstone of my leadership development programs.

Boyatzis, Smith & Van Oosten

Coaching with compassion over compliance. The research on what actually helps people change long-term changed how I approach every coaching conversation.

Michael Bungay Stanier

The Coaching Habit

Seven questions that make you a better coach and a better leader. Short, practical, and immediately usable. I recommend it to every people manager I work with.

For Individuals

1:1 Integrative Coaching

Quiet the mental noise, build emotional intelligence, and lead yourself with intention. Coaching blends EQ work, identity work, and behaviour change into one grounded process.

Three phases. One through-line: you.

Phase 01

Uncover

Before anything changes, you have to see it clearly. We use reflective inquiry and EQ tools to surface the beliefs, patterns, and stories running the show, often just outside your awareness.

Phase 02

Unravel

Identity work is the heart of this phase. We separate inherited patterns from your actual values and desires. Who were you told to be, and who do you actually want to be?

Phase 03

Unleash

With clarity comes capacity. Building the grounded confidence and decision-making habits that let you move forward, not despite who you are, but fully because of it.
