The Methodology

How I design learning.

Listen first. Design for real scenarios. Measure behaviour change.

Priority 01

Learning experiences people love

I design for engagement, relevance, and real-world application over passive content.

Real scenariosLearner choicePractice over passive
In practiceIf learners are bored, nothing else matters. I turn real workplace decisions into scenarios where learners choose, see the consequence, and try again. The experience becomes rehearsal for the job rather than information about the job.
Priority 02

Outcomes the business can measure

Every program starts from a business goal and ends with evidence of impact.

Needs analysisMetrics by designBehaviour change
In practiceLearning is an investment, and I treat it like one. I define the behaviour and business signal before building content. For onboarding, that can mean time to proficiency, quality scores, manager confidence, and evidence that people apply the skill after training.

A coaching conversation

I use coaching conversations to turn reflection into clarity, awareness, and meaningful action.

01
01 · Connect

Build trust and presence

I create a grounded, psychologically safe space, establish rapport, and meet the person where they are before moving into the work.

02
02 · Goal

Establish the goal

We clarify what the client wants from the conversation and what would make the time feel genuinely valuable.

03
03 · Explore

Explore the current reality

I listen deeply, reflect what I notice, and ask questions that help the client understand the situation, emotions, patterns, and context more fully.

04
04 · Awareness

Generate awareness

We challenge assumptions, notice beliefs, reframe perspectives, and make room for insights that expand what the client can see.

05
05 · Options

Create options

The client generates possibilities and explores different ways forward. My role is to invite thinking, not prescribe advice.

06
06 · Action

Commit to action

The client chooses a next step that feels meaningful, realistic, and aligned with their goals and values.

07
07 · Close

Capture learning and accountability

We reflect on insights, confidence, commitments, and what support or accountability will help the client carry the learning forward.
